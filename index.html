100% On-Device Privacy Guaranteed

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Application: AudioScribe: Voice to Text AI ("AudioScribe")

Summary: AudioScribe operates completely offline. Your voice recordings, imported audio files, and generated transcripts never leave your mobile device. We do not operate any backend cloud servers that receive or store your audio.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Voice Recordings: All speech-to-text processing is performed locally on your device hardware using embedded on-device AI models. Your audio is never sent over the internet.
  • No Transcriptions or Notes: All transcribed text, notes, and AI summaries remain stored strictly in your device's local application sandbox.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require registration, login accounts, email addresses, names, or phone numbers to use AudioScribe.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

AudioScribe uses reputable third-party SDKs to provide free access, advertising, and subscription billing:

  • Google AdMob: For non-Pro users, Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) and performance analytics to serve contextual or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely through Google Play. We do not collect or have access to your credit card or financial billing information.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Record Audio (Microphone): Required strictly to capture live speech for local transcription. We never transmit audio to any remote servers.
  • Storage / Media: Required solely to import audio files you select from your device and to save your exported transcriptions (PDF, Word, TXT, SRT) to your Downloads folder.
  • Internet Access: Used strictly to download optional offline AI models on demand (from HuggingFace/Firebase CDN), serve AdMob ads (for free tier), and verify Google Play subscriptions.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all transcription data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and total control over your data. You can delete individual audio recordings or transcripts at any time within the app, or clear all data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

AudioScribe is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
